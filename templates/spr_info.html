Distribution systems

Energy.gov

Seaway

Bryan Mound connects to refinery hubs in Houston, Texas City, and Freeport.

Texoma

Big Hill and West Hackberry connect to Beaumont-Port Arthur, Lake Charles, New Orleans, and limited Houston outlets.

Capline

Bayou Choctaw connects to Baton Rouge area refineries and one New Orleans area refinery.

The SPR also connects to three contracted marine terminals with 2.220 MMbbl/d capacity and one DOE-owned St. James terminal with 400 Mbbl/d capacity.

How withdrawal works

DOE storage sites

SPR crude sits in underground salt caverns. During a drawdown, fresh water is pumped into the bottom of a cavern, displacing crude oil to the surface.

After the oil reaches the surface, pipelines and terminals move it into refinery hubs, interstate crude pipelines, or marine terminals. This is why site, delivery point, and rate limits matter for every tender.

The current mockup treats site drawdown as a physical constraint and delivery points as the link between DOE awards and refinery access.
