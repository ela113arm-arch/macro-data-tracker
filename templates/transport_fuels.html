Sources & conversion methodology

Monthly volumes are divided by calendar days and standardized to M b/d before calculating change versus the same month a year earlier. Mass conversions: gasoline 8.50, jet fuel 7.77, diesel 7.23 barrels/tonne; volume conversion: 6.28981077 barrels/m³. The black line adds the three product changes. Mixed latest months must not be treated as a single-month global total.
